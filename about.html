Our Story

Pretty Bags of Rocks is a small polished-rock shop based in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan. Every stone we sell begins the same way it always has — collected by hand, one at a time, and chosen because something about it caught the eye: a swirl of colour, an interesting pattern, a shape that feels just right in the palm of your hand.

From there, each stone is tumbled with patience through weeks of careful stages, until a rough, unassuming rock emerges smooth, glossy, and full of the beauty that was hiding inside it all along. I started Pretty Bags of Rocks to share that moment — the quiet delight of holding a freshly polished stone up to the light — with fellow rock lovers across Canada.

Meet the Maker

About the Owner

My love of rocks started when I was young, collecting stones for my grandpa to polish. I would bring him my best finds, and he would tumble them until they shone. Those polished stones felt like treasure — and honestly, they still do.

These days, the tradition has come full circle: now my daughter and I do the collecting together, and I'm the one who does the polishing. I have always loved the way rocks look, and I wanted to be able to share the beauty of a freshly polished stone with others — that's how this little shop began.

Outside the tumbler, I'm a wife and a mom of two, a full-time student, and a devoted plant and animal lover. I'm currently studying to become a funeral director and embalmer, while also working full-time at a funeral home. Life is full, in the very best way.

Rock tumbling is my gentle, passive hobby — it brings me real joy, one polished stone at a time.
